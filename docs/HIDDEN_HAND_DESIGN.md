# Hidden Hand Design Document  
**Epochs of Ascendancy**

**Version:** 1.0 (Compiled)  
**Status:** Design Phase – Ready for Review & Iteration

---

## 1. Overview & Concept

The **Hidden Hand** (also referred to as “the Veil”) is a secret transnational faction that operates in the background of the normal nation-state system. It is not a country — it is a network of influence that seeks to gradually centralize global power under a unified world order.

It maintains three primary power centers:
- **City of London** — Financial & Economic Control
- **Vatican** — Ideological, Religious & Soft Power
- **Washington D.C.** — Military, Intelligence & Political Enforcement

The Hidden Hand does **not** seek total direct control. Instead, it prefers to **guide, manipulate, and subtly steer** nations, leaders, and events toward its long-term goals while maintaining plausible deniability.

---

## 2. Core Goals

- Gradual **globalization** and erosion of strong national sovereignty
- Creation of **supranational institutions** that supersede nation-states
- Controlled **conflict and division** (keep major powers in competition or low-level wars so they remain dependent)
- Support **puppet leaders and movements** that advance centralization
- Economic dominance through central banking, debt, and global finance
- Gradual shift toward a **de facto one-world government** (effective control, not necessarily overt)

They are **not** cartoonishly evil — they genuinely believe their vision creates stability and long-term progress. This makes them more interesting and harder to simply “fight.”

---

## 3. Power Centers & Specialties

| Power Center       | Primary Domain              | Key Levers                                      | Signature Actions                          |
|--------------------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| **City of London** | Finance & Trade             | Central banks, debt, global finance, black markets | Economic crises, currency manipulation, trade deals that favor centralization |
| **Vatican**        | Ideology & Soft Power       | Religion, culture, NGOs, moral authority        | Shaping public opinion, influencing leaders through faith networks, cultural movements |
| **Washington D.C.**| Military & Intelligence     | U.S. military as proxy force, intelligence networks | Proxy wars, regime change, intelligence operations, enforcing economic sanctions |

## 4. Core Mechanics

### Influence System
Every major country has a hidden **Hidden Hand Influence** score (0–100). Higher influence = more likely that country takes actions favorable to the Hidden Hand (even if suboptimal for them).

Influence can be increased through:
- Economic pressure
- Political infiltration
- Cultural influence
- Blackmail
- Successful background actions

Players (and AI) can try to **reduce** influence through exposure, reforms, strong independent leadership, or counter-espionage.

### Background Actions
The Hidden Hand performs most of its work through indirect, deniable actions. These should feel sinister but plausible and mostly happen **off-screen** unless the player has invested in counter-intelligence.

Major categories include:
- **Economic Warfare** (debt traps, currency attacks, black market operations)
- **Technology Control** (stealing technology, selling it to rivals, suppressing breakthroughs)
- **Political Manipulation** (funding parties, regime change, assassinations, fomenting riots and civil unrest)
- **Information & Narrative Control** (media capture, celebrity influence, narrative shaping)
- **Biological, Chemical & Atmospheric Operations** (engineered diseases, water/food contamination, atmospheric spraying, compliance-enhancing agents)
- **Proxy War & False Flag Operations** (pressuring or manipulating countries into war via false flags, planted WMD evidence, staged incidents)
- **Regime Change & Targeted Eliminations**

## 5. Prestige vs Hidden Hand Alignment

These are **two separate but interacting systems**:

| System                        | What It Represents                              | How It’s Gained / Lost                              | Strategic Tension |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Prestige / World Standing** | General international reputation and soft power | Wars, economic success, diplomacy, scientific achievements | High prestige does **not** automatically mean high Hidden Hand favor |
| **Hidden Hand Alignment**     | How much the Hidden Hand supports or controls you | Following their policies, accepting “help”, allowing influence to grow | Playing along gives short-term advantages but costs long-term sovereignty |

A country can have **high prestige but low Hidden Hand alignment** (a strong, respected independent power) or **low prestige but high alignment** (a propped-up puppet).

---

## 6. Hidden / Discoverable Nature (Core Design Principle)

The Hidden Hand should feel like a **true hidden layer** for most players.

**Key Rules:**
- At game start, almost no one has direct visibility into Hidden Hand influence or actions.
- Information is **gated behind discovery**.
- Most players only become aware through indirect clues, major events, or deliberate investigation.

**Discovery Progression:**
- **Stage 1 – Unaware**: The system runs entirely in the background.
- **Stage 2 – Suspicion**: Player notices strange patterns (wars that don’t make sense, perfectly timed crises, coordinated international responses).
- **Stage 3 – Awareness**: Player gains access to partial influence reports.
- **Stage 4 – Active Engagement**: Player can deliberately increase or decrease influence, support or oppose the Hidden Hand, or attempt to expose it.

## 7. Player Interaction & Tools

### Ways to Increase Alignment (Play Along)
- Accept “generous” loans or economic packages
- Appoint compromised ministers or advisors
- Join international organizations or treaties the Hidden Hand is pushing
- Allow foreign military bases or intelligence cooperation
- Suppress movements that oppose globalization

### Ways to Resist or Reduce Influence
- Pass strong anti-corruption or transparency laws
- Expose operations through high-level espionage
- Support truly independent leaders and movements
- Maintain high economic self-sufficiency
- Build alternative alliances that bypass Hidden Hand-controlled institutions
- Publicly denounce their influence (risky but high reward in Prestige)

### Retaliation
If a country reduces Hidden Hand influence too aggressively, they may face:
- Sudden economic crises or currency attacks
- Political scandals or “color revolutions”
- Proxy conflicts or insurgencies
- Loss of access to certain international financial systems

---

## 8. Internal Competition Between the Three Power Centers

While the three centers share the broad goal of centralized global control, they **compete with each other** for dominance within that vision.

**Core Dynamic:**
- They disagree on **which center should be the primary seat of power** in the new world order.
- This creates internal rivalries, shifting alliances, and occasional sabotage between the centers.

**Mechanical Differentiation:**

| Power Center       | Primary Goal Within the Hidden Hand     | Signature Methods                     | Internal Rivalries                          | Mechanical Flavor |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| **City of London** | Financial institutions at the center    | Debt, central banking, black markets  | Competes with Washington over financial vs military dominance | Strong long-term economic influence |
| **Vatican**        | Ideological and cultural authority      | Narrative control, religious networks | Clashes with pure financial or military approaches | Excellent at long-term cultural shaping |
| **Washington D.C.**| Military and intelligence power         | Proxy wars, regime change, intelligence | Resents being used as muscle for London’s goals | Best at direct kinetic action and rapid power projection |

**Gameplay Implication:** The player can potentially exploit these divisions once they have enough awareness.

## 9. Playing as One of the Three Power Centers (Late / Alternate Gameplay)

After the player has completed one or more full campaigns and reached certain milestones (high discovery of the Hidden Hand, major world events, etc.), they can unlock the ability to **play as one of the three power centers** directly.

### Key Differences from Normal Country Play
- You no longer control a single nation. You influence many countries indirectly.
- Gameplay is heavily **event-driven, influence-based, and subtle**.
- Primary resources are **Influence, Agents, Narrative Control, and Economic Leverage** rather than traditional industry or military production.
- You have access to the full suite of Background Actions (false flags, economic warfare, technological manipulation, etc.), but with higher risk of catastrophic blowback if discovered.
- Victory conditions are different (e.g. achieving dominant control over the other two centers, successfully creating a stable global governance structure, surviving a major exposure crisis, etc.).

### Why This Works Well
- Gives veteran players a completely different way to experience the game.
- Makes the Hidden Hand feel like a real, playable faction rather than just an antagonist.
- Creates strong replayability and a “New Game+” style experience.
- Fits thematically with the idea that the Hidden Hand operates on a different layer than normal nations.

This mode would be unlocked after significant progress in normal campaigns and would represent a major shift in gameplay perspective.

---

## 10. Victory Paths & Long-term Fantasy

**Success for the Hidden Hand** looks like:
- Major supranational organizations gain real power over nations
- Most major economies are deeply interdependent and vulnerable to central control
- Strong nationalist or independent movements are marginalized
- A major crisis occurs that justifies further centralization

**Player Victory Paths Involving the Hidden Hand:**
- **Join Them**: Work with the Hidden Hand to help build the new world order (you become very powerful but lose true sovereignty).
- **Resist Them**: Build a strong, independent power bloc that can stand against their influence.
- **Expose & Dismantle**: Focus on intelligence, research, and political warfare to weaken and reveal the Hidden Hand over time.
- **Play Both Sides**: Maintain plausible deniability while extracting benefits while slowly reducing their control.
- **Play as One of the Three Centers** (late/alternate mode): Compete for dominance within the Hidden Hand itself.
